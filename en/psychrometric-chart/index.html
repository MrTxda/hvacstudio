Calculation method & reading the chart ＋

The horizontal axis is dry-bulb temperature; the vertical axis is humidity ratio per kg of dry air. The boundary curve is 100% RH saturation. Toggle relative humidity, enthalpy, wet-bulb and specific-volume lines. Chart temperature range: −20 to 60 °C; the humidity axis expands to include valid points.

Saturation vapor pressure follows Hyland–Wexler/ASHRAE. W = 0.621945·Pv/(P−Pv); h = 1.006·T + W(2501 + 1.86·T). Wet bulb and dew point are solved iteratively. Below 0 °C, saturation and dew point use equilibrium over ice (frost point).

Heat transfer rate A → B = ṁda(hB−hA); moisture addition/removal rate = ṁda(WB−WA). Mixing balances enthalpy and water vapor using dry-air mass fractions. Supersaturated mixtures are flagged; condensation or fog separation is not modeled. RH = 0 has no finite dew point.

Results support preliminary calculations and require verification before use. View terms of use.
