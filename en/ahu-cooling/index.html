Results

Method & scope

Each volume flow is entered at its own temperature, RH and pressure. ṁda = Qv/(3600v); mixing conserves dry-air mass, moisture and enthalpy. Leaving volume flow can differ from total inlet volume because air properties change.

Total duty = ṁda(hM − hL). Sensible duty is defined as ṁda(1.006 + 1.86WM)(TM − TL); latent duty is the remainder. Condensate = ṁda(WM − WL) × 3600 kg/h. Leaving condensate enthalpy is neglected.

Chilled water: Qv,w = Q/(ρw cp,w ΔTw) × 3600. Actual-duty and allowance-duty flows are separate; allowance does not increase condensate for the specified air states. Pure water only; glycol is unsupported.

Leaving air is a user-specified target, not verified against ADP, bypass factor, coil rows or area. Reheat, fan heat, recovery, leakage, pressure losses, room loads and multizone ventilation efficiency are excluded. No AHU/FAHU product selection or standards-compliance determination.
