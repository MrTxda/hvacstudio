ABOUT HVAC STUDIO

One toolkit.
For everyday HVAC calculations.

HVAC Studio is a free calculation website for HVAC and M&E engineers and students. It supports preliminary calculations, input checks and project-based records, from cooling loads and flow rates to distribution systems, equipment duty and insulation.

Explore the calculators →

Loads & flows

Estimate sensible and latent cooling loads, compare AC capacity and convert cooling duty into design air or water flow.

Air systems

Determine ventilation flow, compare round and rectangular ducts, size grilles and calculate fan pressure and power along a critical path.

Water systems

Compare pipe sizes, calculate pump head for closed or open systems and estimate diaphragm expansion-vessel capacity.

Detailed checks

Inspect individual fitting losses using K and its reference section, check water-pipe, round/rectangular duct and refrigerant-pipe insulation, and explore moist-air states and mixing.

Keep multiple results in one project

Tool inputs and results are stored in your browser. Ducts, pipes, grilles, fittings and insulation support schedules with individual editing and reports. Results remain as saved; recalculate and update them when related inputs change.

Connect calculation steps and keep your data

Transfer cooling loads to flow calculations, send flow to sizing tools and add fitting losses to fan/pump paths. Export JSON for backups or another device; PDF is for reading and sharing. Project data does not currently sync to a server, so keep a backup before clearing browser data.

Understand the result before using it

Each tool states its formulas, assumptions and scope. Cooling loads are preliminary estimates; fan and pump tools calculate duty requirements for catalogue checks. Fitting K values are user-entered. Insulation checks modelled surfaces under chosen conditions and does not replace checks of thermal bridges, joints or vapour barriers.

Use your browser or an app window

Available in English and Vietnamese on desktop and mobile, without an account. Install from Chrome/Edge for quick access in a separate window; an Internet connection is currently needed to load the tools.
