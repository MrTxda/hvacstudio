Method & limitations ＋

Steady-state heat-gain estimate, not hourly HB/RTS. Walls/roof: U·A·(Tout−Tin+ΔTsolar); glazing: U·A·ΔT + A·I·SHGC·Fshade. Solar equivalent rise is user-defined and is not derived from orientation, weather or design hour.

People: count × sensible/latent gain per person. Lighting: area × W/m². Equipment: heat gain × use factor. Outdoor-air total load uses dry-air mass flow × enthalpy difference; sensible load uses heat capacity at indoor humidity ratio, with latent load as the remainder. Cooler/drier outdoor air can produce negative components; aggregate sensible and latent loads are each floored at zero for sizing.

The allowance applies to both sensible and latent loads. The smallest reference BTU/h size meeting total, sensible and latent requirements is selected using unit count, assumed SHR and capacity factor. This does not replace manufacturer selection data or peak-load calculations with weather, thermal storage, solar orientation, thermal bridges and schedules. Floor/adjacent-zone, fan/duct and recovery effects are not calculated; use other gains as appropriate.
