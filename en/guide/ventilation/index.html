Purpose & scope

Calculate ventilation by occupancy, area, ACH or combined occupancy + area/Ez.

Zone/space calculation; excludes multizone system ventilation efficiency, specific contaminants and leakage. Selecting ASHRAE does not load standard table rates.

Inputs

Use drawings, design conditions and manufacturer data. Units and example values follow the calculator; example values are not universal design requirements.

Steps

  1. Select the method and design basis; replace example rates with applicable occupancy/standard values.
  2. Enter floor area, height, occupancy and applicable rates. For combined calculations check Ez configuration.
  3. Read the selected method’s flow; transfer to ducts or outlets as needed.

Method & limitations

Zone/space calculation; excludes multizone system ventilation efficiency, specific contaminants and leakage. Selecting ASHRAE does not load standard table rates.

Default rates are entry examples, not requirements for every occupancy. Enter rates from the occupancy, standard and edition applicable to the project.

Combined method: Vbz = Rp·P + Ra·A; Voz = Vbz/Ez. Air-change method: V = A·h·ACH. The four methods are separate alternatives, not automatically summed or maximized.

The combined result is single-zone outdoor air only. Multizone system efficiency, population diversity, demand control and edition-specific density adjustments are excluded. Not a smoke extraction or stair pressurization calculation.

Common mistakes

Adding independent method results; treating example rates as mandatory; omitting Ez in the combined method.

Projects & reports

  1. Choose or create an engineering project and enter your actual design inputs.
  2. Project inputs/results are autosaved in this browser. Where a saved-list panel is available, name and add the selected section or alternative to compare multiple options.
  3. Review the report before exporting PDF. In the print dialog select Save as PDF. Export JSON for backup and use Open file to restore it; PDF cannot restore project data.

Saved results do not automatically recalculate when another tool changes. Review any dependency warning, recalculate and update the affected results.
