Purpose & scope

Estimate a room’s sensible and latent cooling loads and check equipment capacity against both components.

Single-room steady-state estimate, not hourly HB/RTS. BTU/h sizes are references; verify actual equipment sensible/latent capacities.

Inputs

Use drawings, design conditions and manufacturer data. Units and example values follow the calculator; example values are not universal design requirements.

Steps

  1. Enter room size, indoor/outdoor conditions and actual wall, roof and glazing areas.
  2. Enter occupants, lighting, equipment, ventilation and infiltration without double counting outdoor airflow.
  3. Set unit count, equipment SHR and capacity factor at design conditions; inspect load components before selection.

Method & limitations

Single-room steady-state estimate, not hourly HB/RTS. BTU/h sizes are references; verify actual equipment sensible/latent capacities.

Steady-state heat-gain estimate, not hourly HB/RTS. Walls/roof: U·A·(Tout−Tin+ΔTsolar); glazing: U·A·ΔT + A·I·SHGC·Fshade. Solar equivalent rise is user-defined and is not derived from orientation, weather or design hour.

People: count × sensible/latent gain per person. Lighting: area × W/m². Equipment: heat gain × use factor. Outdoor-air total load uses dry-air mass flow × enthalpy difference; sensible load uses heat capacity at indoor humidity ratio, with latent load as the remainder. Cooler/drier outdoor air can produce negative components; aggregate sensible and latent loads are each floored at zero for sizing.

The allowance applies to both sensible and latent loads. The smallest reference BTU/h size meeting total, sensible and latent requirements is selected using unit count, assumed SHR and capacity factor. This does not replace manufacturer selection data or peak-load calculations with weather, thermal storage, solar orientation, thermal bridges and schedules. Floor/adjacent-zone, fan/duct and recovery effects are not calculated; use other gains as appropriate.

Common mistakes

Confusing cooling capacity with electrical input; including glazing in opaque wall area; substituting room SHR for equipment SHR; double counting ventilation.

Projects & reports

  2. Project inputs/results are autosaved in this browser. Where a saved-list panel is available, name and add the selected section or alternative to compare multiple options.
