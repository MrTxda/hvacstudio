Water pipe sizing

Size/check internal diameters for pressurized water or Manning gravity drainage.

Purpose & scope

Size/check internal diameters for pressurized water or Manning gravity drainage.

Pure water; does not replace glycol, surge or branched-network checks. Manning assumes steady uniform partially full circular-pipe flow; maximum depth ratio 10–80%.

Inputs

Use drawings, design conditions and manufacturer data. Units and example values follow the calculator; example values are not universal design requirements.

Steps

  1. Select chilled water, pressurized water supply or gravity drainage before entering data.
  2. For pressure pipes use internal diameter, temperature, roughness and velocity/Pa/m limits. For drainage use slope, n, depth ratio and velocity limits.
  3. Select a candidate or check an existing pipe, review warnings and save each section.

Method & limitations

Pure water; does not replace glycol, surge or branched-network checks. Manning assumes steady uniform partially full circular-pipe flow; maximum depth ratio 10–80%.

Pressurized pipes: V = Q/A; Re = ρVD/μ; R = fρV²/(2D). Darcy factor uses 64/Re for laminar flow and Colebrook for Re ≥ 4,000. The 2,300–4,000 transition region is interpolated and flagged. Density and viscosity are approximated for pure water at 1–80 °C.

Gravity drainage: Q = A·Rh^(2/3)·√S/n, with Rh = A/P. Normal flow depth in a circular pipe is solved from flow, slope and Manning n. Maximum depth ratio is limited to 10–80% to use the monotonic branch. Only pipes with sufficient capacity and velocities within the minimum and maximum limits are retained.

Straight pipe sections only; excludes fittings, valves, equipment, elevation changes, required residual pressure, unsteady flow and branched networks. Material and design presets are editable reference values.

Common mistakes

Entering DN instead of internal diameter; misreading slope units; assuming hydraulic capacity verifies venting or the whole network.

Projects & reports

  1. Choose or create an engineering project and enter your actual design inputs.
  2. Project inputs/results are autosaved in this browser. Where a saved-list panel is available, name and add the selected section or alternative to compare multiple options.
  3. Review the report before exporting PDF. In the print dialog select Save as PDF. Export JSON for backup and use Open file to restore it; PDF cannot restore project data.

Saved results do not automatically recalculate when another tool changes. Review any dependency warning, recalculate and update the affected results.
