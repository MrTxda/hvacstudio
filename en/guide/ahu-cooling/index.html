Outdoor-air & AHU/FAHU cooling duty

Calculate mixed air, sensible/latent coil duty, condensate and chilled-water flow for a specified leaving-coil state.

Purpose & scope

Calculate mixed air, sensible/latent coil duty, condensate and chilled-water flow for a specified leaving-coil state.

Cooling/dehumidification above 0 °C only; no ADP/BF verification or coil selection. Condensate enthalpy, fan heat, recovery, reheat and pressure losses are excluded.

Inputs

Use drawings, design conditions and manufacturer data. Units and example values follow the calculator; example values are not universal design requirements.

Steps

  1. Choose 100% outdoor-air FAHU or mixed-air AHU; enter pressure and each inlet flow at its own state.
  2. Enter outdoor temperature/RH, return-air conditions if used, and target coil-leaving state.
  3. Enter entering water temperature, water temperature rise, specific heat and allowance. Review actual/allowance duty, condensate and the two distinct water flows.
  4. Transfer results to ducts/fan/pump as needed; obtain coil pressure drops and equipment data from the manufacturer.

Method & limitations

Cooling/dehumidification above 0 °C only; no ADP/BF verification or coil selection. Condensate enthalpy, fan heat, recovery, reheat and pressure losses are excluded.

Each volume flow is entered at its own temperature, RH and pressure. ṁda = Qv/(3600v); mixing conserves dry-air mass, moisture and enthalpy. Leaving volume flow can differ from total inlet volume because air properties change.

Total duty = ṁda(hM − hL). Sensible duty is defined as ṁda(1.006 + 1.86WM)(TM − TL); latent duty is the remainder. Condensate = ṁda(WM − WL) × 3600 kg/h. Leaving condensate enthalpy is neglected.

Chilled water: Qv,w = Q/(ρw cp,w ΔTw) × 3600. Actual-duty and allowance-duty flows are separate; allowance does not increase condensate for the specified air states. Pure water only; glycol is unsupported.

Leaving air is a user-specified target, not verified against ADP, bypass factor, coil rows or area. Reheat, fan heat, recovery, leakage, pressure losses, room loads and multizone ventilation efficiency are excluded. No AHU/FAHU product selection or standards-compliance determination.

Common mistakes

Confusing outdoor-condition flow with supply flow; double counting outdoor-air load; requesting heating/humidification; applying allowance to condensate; confusing allowance water flow with actual-state water flow.
