Purpose & scope

Calculate pump head and power for closed/open water systems from path losses and outlet requirements.

Closed-loop circulation excludes building elevation. Open systems assume an atmospheric source; pressurized source effects and NPSH are not calculated.

Inputs

Use drawings, design conditions and manufacturer data. Units and example values follow the calculator; example values are not universal design requirements.

Steps

  1. Select closed/open system and enter flow, temperature, roughness and efficiencies.
  2. For closed loops include supply and return; use internal diameter and equipment Δp in kPa.
  3. For open systems enter elevation and residual pressure. Verify Q–H, NPSH, operating range and motor against catalogue data.

Method & limitations

Closed-loop circulation excludes building elevation. Open systems assume an atmospheric source; pressurized source effects and NPSH are not calculated.

Enter consecutive sections of the critical path, including supply and return in closed loops. Do not sum parallel branches.

H = Σ(f·L/D + K)·v²/(2g) + equipment head. Closed loops exclude building height from circulation head; fill pressure must be checked separately. Open systems add elevation and residual pressure, assuming an atmospheric source and negligible endpoint velocity-head difference.

Pure water only; not a glycol model. The motor size is a shaft-power guide; verify the pump curve, NPSH, duty point and operating range.

Common mistakes

Adding building height to a closed loop; confusing DN/internal D or kPa/mH₂O; ignoring source pressure and NPSH.
